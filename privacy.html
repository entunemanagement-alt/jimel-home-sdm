Jimel Home SDM — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

Jimel Home SDM is a personal app built and used by one homeowner for his own household. This policy describes what information the app handles and what happens to it.

Information the app accesses

With the homeowner's authorization, the app accesses the homeowner's own Google Home and Nest devices through Google's Smart Device Management API. That can include device names, types and room assignments, device status, camera images and live streams, and device events (such as motion or person notifications) for devices in the homeowner's own home.

How the information is used

The information is used only to show the homeowner his own cameras and devices in his app and to notify him about events in his own home. It is processed on computer equipment the homeowner owns, in his home and on his own servers.

Sharing

The app does not sell information, does not share information with third parties for marketing, and contains no advertising or third-party analytics. Camera and device data travels between Google's device platform (which operates the Nest devices), the homeowner's own equipment, and the homeowner's phone. Note that Nest cameras are cloud-connected devices operated by Google, so their data necessarily passes through Google's platform under Google's own privacy policy.

Storage and retention

Any stored data is kept on the homeowner's own equipment, under his control, for as long as he chooses. He can delete it at any time.

Revoking access

The homeowner can revoke the app's access at any time from his Google Account's third-party access settings, which stops all data flow to the app.
