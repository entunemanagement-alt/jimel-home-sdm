Jimel Home SDM

Jimel Home SDM is a personal, single-household home app. It lets the homeowner view and manage his own Google Home and Nest cameras and devices from his own phone, through a home server he owns and controls.

This app is for the owner's private household use only. It is not a commercial product, it is not offered to the public, and it has no other users.

How it works: with the homeowner's permission, the app connects to the homeowner's own Google account through Google's Smart Device Management API, receives his own devices' information and camera streams, and displays them in his app by way of his own home server.
